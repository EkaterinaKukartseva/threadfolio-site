Questions and answers

Where are my charts stored?

On your device. They are saved automatically after every change and aren’t sent to us.

How do I create a chart?

On the Charts tab, tap the + button, choose a craft and set the grid size.

How do I import a chart from a photo?

When creating a chart, choose a photo. Then select the chart area, tap Detect grid size and set the number of colors. For the best result, use an even, straight-on picture and frame only the grid, without titles.

Why did squares from the legend end up in my chart?

Anything inside the selected area becomes cells. Erase extra cells with the eraser or make the area smaller.

How do I add a chart from the catalog?

On the Catalog tab, tap Add. A copy appears in your charts, and you can edit it as your own.

How do I export a chart?

In the editor, open the ⋯ menu and choose Description, Notes and Export. The chart is saved as a PNG image with its legend.

Will I lose my charts if I delete the app?

Yes, deleting the app deletes its charts from the device, unless you restore them from a device backup.

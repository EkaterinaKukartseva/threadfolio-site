Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Threadfolio doesn’t collect your personal data. The app works without an account and without an internet connection.

Data on your device

Your charts, notes, photos you add to charts and app settings are stored only on your device. We have no access to them. They may be included in your device backups, depending on your iCloud or computer backup settings.

Photos

The app gets only the photos you choose yourself in the system picker. It doesn’t see the rest of your library. Photos are processed on your device and aren’t sent anywhere.

Analytics and advertising

The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking, and doesn’t share data with third parties.

Contacting support

If you write to us, we receive what you put in your email: your address, your message and any attachments. The draft created by the app also includes the app version, the iOS version and the device model. You can see and delete this information before sending. We use your email only to reply to you and improve the app. Emails are stored in our Gmail mailbox, provided by Google.

Deleting your data

Delete a chart in the app, or delete the app to remove all its data from your device. To have your emails deleted, write to us at the address below.

Children

The app doesn’t knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If the way the app handles data changes, we’ll update this page and the date above.
